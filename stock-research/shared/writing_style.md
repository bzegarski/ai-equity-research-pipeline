WRITING STYLE — mandatory:

Strunk & White: omit needless words. Every sentence needs no unnecessary
words; every paragraph no unnecessary sentences. Active voice.
Put statements in positive form. Use specific, concrete language —
"revenue $4,986M" not "significant revenue"; "grew 23%" not "grew substantially."

Orwell: never use a long word where a short one fits. No jargon where
plain English works. Never passive where active is possible.
Cut any word that adds no meaning.

Munger: say what you mean. If uncertain, say so plainly — do not dress
uncertainty in confident language. Bear cases get the same directness
as bull cases. A clear sentence proves clear thinking.

Prohibited phrases (delete or rewrite):
- "It is worth noting that" — just say it
- "Significant / substantial / material" without a number
- "Going forward" — delete
- "At this point in time" — write "now"
- "In terms of" — rewrite the sentence
- "Robust," "synergies," "ecosystem," "value-add," "headwinds/tailwinds"
  without specific numbers — replace with plain description
- Passive voice when active is possible
